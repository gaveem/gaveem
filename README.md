# Hi there, I'm Gaveem 👋

I am a passionate **English Language Coach** and **Visual Content Specialist** with over 20 years of experience across project management, monitoring & evaluation, and educational content design. 

I specialize in empowering adult learners, non-native speakers, and working professionals to master functional English for career advancement. Concurrently, I explore the intersections of cognitive linguistics, semiotics, regional history, and modern technology.
